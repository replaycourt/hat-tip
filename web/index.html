Ledger Rules

Peer thanks, paid by the community’s own DAO

Every round, each member hands out 100 thanks. When it closes, the café’s DAO pays the round’s budget by the thanks received.

Small payouts to members with a record go out at once. Bigger ones, and most first ones, wait for the council. Every point of reputation traces to a round and the people who gave it.
