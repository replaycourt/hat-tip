    Payouts of round –

    Each payout is a proposal in the café’s Sputnik DAO. The receiver’s record decides how many people must say yes.

    1Paid automatically

    Within the receiver’s auto limit: the contract’s own vote passes it.

    2Needs one council vote

    Within the co-sign limit: any one council member can pass or veto it.

    3Needs a council majority

    Larger, or a first payout: a council majority decides.

    Bounties

    Jobs the café can name in advance, posted as Sputnik DAO bounties: a member posts one, a council majority approves the money, a member claims it with a bond and files the work, and one council vote pays it from the treasury. Its poster then thanks the finisher in that round.

    –Open

    Approved by the council, waiting for someone to claim them; new ones in the vote.

    –Being worked on

    Claimed with a bond. Filed work waits for one council vote.

    –Done and paid

    The DAO paid the amount to the finisher and returned the bonds.

    Which approval flows exist, and which Hat Tip adds

    In Sputnik v2 already, used as they are

    • Roles (a group of accounts, token holders, everyone), with permissions per proposal kind and action (transfer:AddProposal, call:VoteApprove).
    • A vote policy per role and kind, as a ratio or a weight, and a default one: the three tiers are three of them.
    • Transfer and FunctionCall proposals, the bond, expiry and Finalize, VoteRemove.
    • Bounties (AddBounty, bounty_claim, BountyDone) for work posted before it is done, with the claim bond: live here, next to the thanks.

    Added by Hat Tip, outside the DAO

    • Amount limits. Sputnik has none, so the contract’s code picks the tier, and the contract has no keys.
    • Rounds of thanks, the counting, reputation and strikes.
    • Every payout filed as a DAO proposal, its outcome read back (pay, check_payout).

    Set in the DAO’s policy

    • The factory’s auto-update off, so nobody can replace the DAO’s code.
    • Members may post bounties and file their work, nothing else; one council vote approves finished work, a majority any new bounty.
